A turn-based grand-strategy game on a hex grid. Move armies, hold supply lines, take
cities, and decide who the next war is with.

CAMPAIGN
Five training exercises, each built around one idea: an amphibious crossing, a supply
line stretched across a desert, an island chain that can only be taken by sea and air,
a defensive battle won by not losing, and a mountain offensive your vehicles cannot
follow you into. Finish faster for more stars; stars pay out medals you spend on
commanders who then serve you in every later game.

CONQUEST
The whole world map, a hundred-odd nations, everyone at peace on turn one. Pick anyone
— a superpower or a country with a single city — and take 80% of the planet.

WHAT MAKES A BATTLE
Every unit has four separate attack values: against infantry, armour, ships and
aircraft. Anti-tank guns shred armour and bounce off infantry. Fighters own the sky and
do nothing on the ground. Artillery strikes from range and takes no return fire, but is
nearly helpless once something reaches it. Terrain, city walls and entrenchment all
protect the defender, so where you fight matters as much as what you bring.

SUPPLY IS THE REAL OPPONENT
Supply spreads out from your own cities along your own territory, and mountains cost
more to reach across than plains do. An army that outruns its logistics loses strength
every turn and eventually starves. Supply trucks and headquarters carry a small bubble
with them — that is how an offensive keeps moving.

20 unit types across land, sea and air. Six research branches. 16 commanders with
stacking skills. Diplomacy with war, peace and alliances.

FREE SOFTWARE
GPL-3.0-or-later. No ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases, and no Android permissions
at all — the game never touches the network, your storage or your location. Traditional
Chinese, Simplified Chinese and English, switchable inside the app.
